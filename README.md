I am a consultant engineer in hydraulic engineering and geoinformatics, with 19+ years of independent practice for the Greek government, developers and construction firms. My research applies statistics and machine learning to hydrology, water resources and land administration. I am a member of the Technical Chamber of Greece and of the Hellenic Association of Rural and Surveying Engineers.

## Research interests

- **Hydrology and hydrometeorology:** rainfall erosivity, extreme rainfall, design storms and hyetographs
- **Water resources management:** sediment transport, groundwater allocation, water distribution networks
- **Geoinformatics and cadastre:** positional accuracy improvement of cadastral and historical maps
- **Machine learning and statistics:** clustering, supervised learning, extreme value analysis, mostly in R
